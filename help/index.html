Get help

If stress is making it hard to work, sleep or enjoy things for more than two weeks, talk to a doctor or a counsellor. You do not have to wait for it to get worse.

In an emergency

If you or someone else is in danger, call 1990 for an ambulance or 119 for the police.

1990 119
